Daily Eye Care Reminders

🧼

Wash Your Hands

Always wash your hands before touching your eyes

👀

20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds

🕶️

Wear Sunglasses

Protect your eyes from UV rays outdoors

💄

Remove Makeup

Always remove makeup before sleeping every night

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily

🏥

Regular Check-ups

Get a complete eye check-up every year

🥗

Healthy Diet

Eat fruits and green vegetables for eye health

😴

Proper Sleep

Sleep 7-8 hours daily to rest your eyes

⚠️ Never Ignore These Symptoms

  • Blurry vision or sudden vision changes
  • Severe eye pain or persistent discomfort
  • Redness that doesn't go away
  • Seeing flashes of light or floaters
  • Double vision

Consult an eye doctor immediately if you experience any of these symptoms.
